## SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW AND CORE VALUES

### 1.1 About SteelCore Industries Limited

SteelCore Industries Limited is a fully integrated steel manufacturing organization with operations spanning Blast Furnace production, Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) steelmaking, Hot Rolling Mills, Cold Rolling Mills, Coating Lines, and downstream processing facilities. The company employs over 18,000 permanent employees and 6,000 contract workers across its manufacturing campuses.

SteelCore is committed to sustainable steel manufacturing, digital transformation, and developing a highly skilled, safety-first workforce capable of meeting the demands of modern integrated steel production.

### 1.2 Core Values

SteelCore's organizational culture is built on five foundational values that govern every aspect of how employees, managers, and leaders conduct themselves:

| Value | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Safety First** | No production target justifies compromising the safety of any employee, contractor, or visitor. Safety is a non-negotiable condition of employment. |
| **Integrity** | We act with honesty and transparency in all professional interactions, decisions, and communications. |
| **Excellence** | We pursue continuous improvement in every process, product, and person. Excellence is a daily commitment, not an annual event. |
| **Collaboration** | We respect diverse perspectives and leverage collective intelligence across departments, functions, and seniority levels. |
| **Innovation** | We embrace digital manufacturing, AI adoption, and technological advancement as essential drivers of future competitiveness. |

All employees are expected to embody these values in their daily conduct. Performance appraisals, promotion evaluations, and disciplinary proceedings will assess alignment with these values as a core criterion.

## SECTION 2: CODE OF CONDUCT

### 2.1 Professional Standards

Every SteelCore employee, regardless of grade, department, or location, is expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct. This includes:

- **Respect and Dignity:** All employees shall treat colleagues, supervisors, contractors, and visitors with respect. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, or intimidation on the basis of gender, religion, caste, nationality, age, or disability is strictly prohibited and shall result in immediate disciplinary action.

- **Confidentiality:** Employees shall not disclose proprietary process information, customer data, financial data, or intellectual property to external parties without explicit written authorization. This obligation continues after separation from employment.

- **Conflict of Interest:** Employees must disclose any financial interest, personal relationship, or outside employment that may create a conflict of interest with SteelCore's business activities. Disclosures must be submitted to the HR Business Partner in writing.

- **Use of Company Resources:** Company assets — including IT systems, vehicles, tools, equipment, and intellectual property — shall be used exclusively for authorized business purposes.

- **Social Media:** Employees must not post confidential company information, process data, or statements that could harm the company's reputation on any public platform. Internal communications and operational details are classified as confidential.

### 2.2 Anti-Corruption and Gifts Policy

Employees in procurement, contracting, vendor management, and business development roles must comply with SteelCore's Zero Tolerance Anti-Corruption Policy:

- Gifts, hospitality, or entertainment valued above ₹2,000 from vendors, suppliers, or contractors must be disclosed to the Ethics Committee within 48 hours of receipt.
- Facilitation payments of any amount are prohibited.
- Violations will result in immediate termination and referral to legal authorities.

### 2.3 Digital and IT Conduct

- Employees shall not install unauthorized software on company devices.
- All data stored on company systems is subject to periodic audit.
- Cybersecurity incidents or suspicious activities must be reported to the IT Helpdesk within 2 hours of discovery.
- Access credentials shall not be shared under any circumstances.

## SECTION 3: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE POLICY

### 3.1 Standard Working Hours

SteelCore operates across multiple shift patterns to support 24/7 production requirements. Standard working hours by employee category are as follows:

| Employee Category | Standard Hours | Days Per Week |
|---|---|---|
| Staff and Management | 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM | Monday to Saturday (5.5 days) |
| General Shift (Non-Production) | 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Monday to Saturday (5 days) |
| Rotating Shift — Shift A | 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM | As per Shift Roster |
| Rotating Shift — Shift B | 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM | As per Shift Roster |
| Rotating Shift — Shift C | 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM | As per Shift Roster |

Total contractual working hours for permanent employees: **48 hours per week** (inclusive of one 30-minute paid meal break per shift).

### 3.2 Attendance Policy

- Attendance is recorded via biometric systems at all plant entry and exit points.
- Late arrival beyond 15 minutes will be marked as Half-Day Absence unless prior approval has been obtained from the reporting manager.
- Three or more consecutive unexplained absences will trigger a formal inquiry by the HR Business Partner.
- Persistent absenteeism (defined as more than 10 days of unauthorized absence in a rolling 12-month period) will be referred to the Disciplinary Committee.

### 3.3 Overtime

- Overtime for shift workers is compensated at 1.5x the standard hourly rate.
- Overtime on declared National Holidays and Sundays is compensated at 2x the standard hourly rate.
- All overtime must be pre-approved by the Department Head. Post-facto claims will not be entertained.

### 3.4 Work From Home (WFH)

WFH eligibility applies exclusively to staff roles in the following departments: HR, Finance, IT, Supply Chain, Data Analytics, AI & Digital Transformation, and Quality (non-production roles only). Production, Maintenance, Safety, Mechanical, Electrical, Automation, Blast Furnace, EAF, and Rolling Mill employees are not eligible for WFH.

- Eligible employees may work remotely for a maximum of **2 days per week**.
- WFH is subject to manager approval and operational requirements.
- Employees on WFH remain fully accountable for attendance, deliverables, and response time.

## SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY

### 4.1 Types of Leave

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave (EL) | 30 days per year | Accumulated at 2.5 days/month. Minimum 3 days advance notice required. Max encashment of 60 days at retirement. |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 10 days per year | Non-accumulating. Max 3 consecutive days per instance. Cannot be combined with EL. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days per year | Medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days. |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | As per Maternity Benefit Act 1961. Applicable to all female permanent employees. |
| Paternity Leave | 5 days | Must be taken within 30 days of child birth or adoption. |
| Bereavement Leave | 3 days | Applicable for first-degree relatives (spouse, parents, children). |
| Marriage Leave | 3 days | Applicable once during tenure of employment. |
| Compensatory Off | As accrued | For approved weekend or holiday work. Must be consumed within 60 days. |

### 4.2 Leave Application Process

1. All leave applications must be submitted via the Employee Self-Service Portal (ESSP) at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave.
2. Emergency leave must be communicated to the reporting manager via phone or message within 1 hour of start of shift.
3. Leave approval rests with the reporting manager in consultation with the Department Head for absences exceeding 5 days.
4. Unapproved leave will be treated as Loss of Pay (LOP) and may attract disciplinary action.

### 4.3 National and Festival Holidays

SteelCore observes **12 National and Festival Holidays** per calendar year. A holiday calendar is published by HR at the beginning of each financial year. Production departments shall identify minimum staffing requirements for each holiday in advance.

## SECTION 5: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

### 5.1 Overview

SteelCore's Performance Management System (PMS) is a continuous performance dialogue framework that runs on an annual cycle with quarterly check-ins. The purpose of the PMS is to:

- Align individual goals with departmental and organizational objectives.
- Identify high performers, core contributors, and employees requiring additional support.
- Provide a fair, transparent, and documented basis for promotion, compensation, and learning decisions.

### 5.2 Performance Cycle

| Phase | Period | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Goal Setting | April 1 – April 30 | Employee and manager agree on SMART goals aligned to department KPIs |
| Mid-Year Review | October 1 – October 15 | Progress discussion, goal adjustment if required, feedback exchange |
| Annual Appraisal | March 1 – March 31 | Full-year performance assessment and rating |
| Calibration | April 1 – April 15 | Department Head calibration of ratings across the team |
| Communication | April 16 – April 30 | Rating communication and increment letters issued |

### 5.3 Performance Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals. Demonstrates outstanding initiative and impact beyond role. |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Achieves all goals and exceeds most. A consistent high performer. |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Achieves all agreed goals. A reliable contributor. |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | Achieves some goals. Requires active development and support. |
| 1 | Does Not Meet Expectations | Fails to meet most goals. Placed on Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). |

### 5.4 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

Employees rated 1 (Does Not Meet Expectations) will be enrolled in a structured 90-day PIP by the HR Business Partner and reporting manager. The PIP will:

- Define specific, measurable improvement targets.
- Schedule bi-weekly check-ins.
- Specify consequences if targets are not achieved within the plan period.

Employees on an active PIP are not eligible for promotion, lateral movement, or merit increments during the PIP period.

### 5.5 360-Degree Feedback

Employees at Grade 7 and above (Senior Engineer and above) are subject to annual 360-degree feedback from peers, direct reports, and cross-functional stakeholders. 360-degree feedback scores inform but do not solely determine the final performance rating.

## SECTION 6: PROMOTION ELIGIBILITY AND CAREER GROWTH

### 6.1 Promotion Philosophy

SteelCore promotes individuals based on demonstrated competency, sustained performance, leadership readiness, and organizational need. Promotions are not automatic or time-based. They require a combination of:

- Minimum time in current grade.
- Performance rating threshold.
- Competency assessment against the next grade's requirements.
- Availability of a position at the next grade level.

### 6.2 Promotion Eligibility Summary

| From Grade | To Grade | Minimum Time in Grade | Minimum Performance Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade 1 – Trainee | Grade 2 – Junior Technician | 1 year | 3 (Meets Expectations) |
| Grade 2 – Junior Technician | Grade 3 – Technician | 2 years | 3 (Meets Expectations) |
| Grade 3 – Technician | Grade 4 – Senior Technician | 2 years | 4 (Exceeds Expectations) |
| Grade 4 – Senior Technician | Grade 5 – Engineer | 2 years | 4 (Exceeds Expectations) |
| Grade 5 – Engineer | Grade 6 – Senior Engineer | 3 years | 4 (Exceeds Expectations) |
| Grade 6 – Senior Engineer | Grade 7 – Specialist | 3 years | 4 (Exceeds Expectations) |
| Grade 7 – Specialist | Grade 8 – Lead/Manager | 4 years | 5 (Exceptional) |

> Full promotion criteria including competency framework requirements, technical assessment, and interview process are detailed in the **Promotion Policy Manual (HR-PROM-002).**

### 6.3 Career Growth Pathways

SteelCore supports two career tracks:

**Technical Track:** For employees who aspire to become deep domain experts without transitioning into people management.

```
Trainee → Junior Technician → Technician → Senior Technician → 
Engineer → Senior Engineer → Specialist → Principal Specialist → Fellow
```

**Leadership Track:** For employees who aspire to manage teams, projects, and departments.

```
Trainee → Junior Technician → Technician → Senior Technician → 
Engineer → Senior Engineer → Team Lead → Manager → Senior Manager → 
Department Head → General Manager
```

## SECTION 7: EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

Every SteelCore employee is responsible for:

### 7.1 Safety Responsibilities

- Wearing mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) at all times in designated areas.
- Reporting unsafe conditions, near-miss incidents, and actual incidents to the Safety department within 1 hour.
- Participating in safety drills, toolbox talks, and mandatory safety training.
- Refusing unsafe work without fear of reprisal.
- Never tampering with safety equipment, emergency systems, or interlocks.

### 7.2 Operational Responsibilities

- Completing assigned tasks to the quality and productivity standards defined for the role.
- Maintaining accurate records in ERP systems, maintenance logs, production reports, and quality checks.
- Participating actively in shift handover briefings and ensuring complete information transfer.
- Escalating process deviations, equipment failures, or quality issues promptly to the supervisor.

### 7.3 Learning and Development Responsibilities

- Completing all mandatory learning modules assigned via the Learning Management System (LMS) within stipulated deadlines.
- Achieving the minimum learning hours target of **40 hours per financial year**.
- Maintaining updated skill profiles in the Employee Self-Service Portal.
- Applying newly acquired skills and knowledge to assigned work.

### 7.4 Ethical Responsibilities

- Reporting fraud, bribery, misconduct, or violations of the Code of Conduct through the Ethics Hotline (1800-222-ETHICS) or directly to the Ethics Committee.
- Cooperating fully and honestly in all internal investigations.
- Maintaining accurate and honest timesheet and expense submissions.

## SECTION 8: TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT EXPECTATIONS

### 8.1 Mandatory Training

The following training programs are mandatory for all employees and must be completed within the specified timelines:

| Training Program | Target Audience | Completion Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Safety Induction | All new joiners | Within 7 days of joining |
| Fire Safety and Emergency Response | All employees | Annual renewal |
| Machine Guarding and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) | All plant employees | Annual renewal |
| Code of Conduct and Anti-Bribery | All employees | Bi-annual renewal |
| Data Security and Cybersecurity Awareness | All employees | Annual renewal |
| Incident Reporting and Near-Miss Awareness | All employees | Annual renewal |

Failure to complete mandatory training within the stipulated deadline will result in a hold on performance rating submission and may affect attendance clearance.

### 8.2 Role-Specific Technical Training

In addition to mandatory training, every employee is expected to complete technical training aligned with their functional competency framework. Role-specific training plans are prepared by Department Heads in coordination with the Learning & Development (L&D) team at the beginning of each financial year.

### 8.3 Learning Budget

- Each employee is eligible for an annual Learning & Development reimbursement of up to **₹75,000 per financial year** for approved external courses, certifications, and professional workshops.
- Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of course completion along with original receipts and a certificate of completion.
- Courses must be pre-approved by the reporting manager and the L&D Business Partner before enrollment.
- Reimbursement is not applicable for courses not included on the Approved Learning Catalog or not pre-approved.

## SECTION 9: MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES

Managers (Grade 8 and above) at SteelCore bear additional accountability for:

### 9.1 People Management

- Conducting timely mid-year and annual performance reviews for all direct reports.
- Providing regular, constructive, and documented feedback at least once per quarter.
- Identifying high-potential employees and nominating them for development programs.
- Managing team attendance, leave approvals, and shift planning effectively.
- Ensuring that new joiners receive proper induction, buddy assignment, and onboarding support within their first 30 days.

### 9.2 Safety Leadership

- Conducting daily safety inspections and toolbox talks at the start of each shift.
- Investigating all near-miss incidents and minor accidents within 24 hours and submitting incident reports to the Safety department.
- Enforcing non-negotiable PPE compliance for every team member at all times.
- Escalating unsafe conditions to the Safety Department immediately.

### 9.3 Team Development

- Creating Individual Development Plans (IDPs) for each direct report in collaboration with the L&D Business Partner.
- Tracking and encouraging progress on learning goals throughout the year.
- Nominating eligible team members for promotion in accordance with the Promotion Policy timeline.
- Supporting knowledge transfer through structured mentoring and internal workshops.

### 9.4 Operational Accountability

- Maintaining shift production targets, quality metrics, and equipment uptime parameters within defined thresholds.
- Ensuring accurate data entry in ERP, MES, and maintenance management systems.
- Participating in root cause analysis (RCA) for all major incidents and process deviations.

## SECTION 10: GRIEVANCE AND DISCIPLINARY PROCESS

### 10.1 Grievance Process

Employees who experience workplace grievances (including harassment, unfair treatment, policy violations, or interpersonal conflicts) may use the following resolution channels:

**Step 1 — Direct Resolution:**
Raise the concern directly with the reporting manager within 10 working days of the incident.

**Step 2 — HR Escalation:**
If unresolved within 5 working days of Step 1, submit a formal written grievance to the HR Business Partner via the ESSP or in person.

**Step 3 — Grievance Committee:**
If unresolved at Step 2, the grievance is referred to the Grievance Committee, which includes the CHRO, Department Head, and an independent committee member. The committee will conduct a structured inquiry and provide a written resolution within 21 working days.

**Step 4 — External Resolution:**
Employees may approach the applicable Labour Commissioner or Industrial Tribunal if dissatisfied with the internal resolution.

All grievances will be treated with confidentiality. Retaliation against an employee for filing a grievance is a disciplinary offense.

### 10.2 Disciplinary Process

Disciplinary action follows a progressive structure:

| Severity | Action |
|---|---|
| Minor misconduct (first instance) | Verbal warning documented by HR |
| Minor misconduct (repeated) | Written warning |
| Moderate misconduct | Final written warning + corrective action plan |
| Gross misconduct | Immediate suspension pending inquiry; termination if substantiated |

Examples of gross misconduct: theft, fraud, physical assault, sexual harassment, deliberate sabotage of equipment, tampering with safety systems.

## SECTION 11: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

**Q1: How do I apply for a promotion?**
> You do not self-apply for promotion. Your manager nominates you through the PMS portal during the annual promotion window (typically February). Ensure your performance ratings and competency assessments are updated before the window opens.

**Q2: Can I carry forward unused leave to the next year?**
> Earned Leave (EL) can be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 90 days. Casual Leave and Sick Leave cannot be carried forward and lapse at year-end.

**Q3: How do I claim training reimbursement?**
> Submit your request via the Learning & Development module in the ESSP within 30 days of course completion. Attach the original payment receipt, course completion certificate, and pre-approval email from your manager.

**Q4: What is the minimum performance rating required to be eligible for promotion?**
> The minimum rating is 4 (Exceeds Expectations) for most grade transitions. For movement to Grade 8 (Manager) and above, a rating of 5 (Exceptional) in the most recent appraisal year is required.

**Q5: Is there a policy for employees pursuing part-time postgraduate education?**
> Yes. Employees pursuing company-approved postgraduate programs in engineering, management, or data science may apply for a Study Leave of up to 10 working days per year in addition to their standard leave entitlement. Study Leave must be pre-approved by the Department Head and HR.

**Q6: How do I raise a safety concern anonymously?**
> You may report safety concerns anonymously via the Safety Helpline: 1800-333-SAFE or through the anonymous reporting option on the ESSP.

**Q7: Who is my HR Business Partner?**
> Each department is assigned a dedicated HR Business Partner. Your HRBP contact can be found on the HR intranet page or by contacting the HR helpdesk at hr.helpdesk@steelcore.in.

**Q8: Can I transfer to a different department?**
> Internal transfers are permitted after a minimum of 2 years in the current role. You must apply through the Internal Mobility portal during open transfer windows (typically May and November each year). Transfers are subject to approval by both the originating and receiving Department Heads.
