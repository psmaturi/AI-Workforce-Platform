## SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE

The Role Competency Matrix (RCM) is a structured reference tool that maps each role at SteelCore Industries Limited to its required competency levels across technical, behavioral, and digital skill areas. It enables:

- **Managers:** To assess current team capability against role requirements.
- **Employees:** To understand the specific skills needed for their current role and the next role in their career path.
- **L&D Team:** To design targeted learning interventions based on identified competency gaps.
- **HR:** To support recruitment, promotion assessments, and succession planning with objective criteria.

### Competency Level Legend

| Code | Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | None | No knowledge or experience required |
| 1 | Awareness | Understands the concept; limited or no application |
| 2 | Practitioner | Applies the skill with some guidance in standard situations |
| 3 | Proficient | Applies independently in complex situations; guides others |
| 4 | Expert | Leading-edge mastery; drives innovation; recognized authority |

## SECTION 2: HOW TO READ THIS MATRIX

Each table shows:
- **Skill Area** in the first column.
- **Required competency level (0–4)** for each role/grade across the top.
- The shaded cell indicates the **target competency level** for that grade.

Employees below the target level have a development gap. Employees at or above the target level are considered competent in that area for their current grade.
