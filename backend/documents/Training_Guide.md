## SECTION 1: LEARNING PHILOSOPHY

SteelCore Industries believes that a highly skilled, continuously learning workforce is the organization's most critical competitive asset. In the era of digital steel manufacturing, where Artificial Intelligence, predictive maintenance, autonomous process control, and data-driven decision making are transforming integrated steel production, every employee must be both technically excellent in their core domain and digitally fluent.

### 1.1 Our Learning Principles

**Learn to Perform:** Every learning intervention must have a direct, measurable impact on role performance, operational safety, or career advancement. We do not train for the sake of training.

**70-20-10 Model:** SteelCore adopts the globally proven 70-20-10 development model:
- **70%** of learning happens through on-the-job experience, stretch assignments, and project work.
- **20%** happens through coaching, mentoring, peer learning, and structured feedback.
- **10%** happens through formal training programs, certifications, and structured courses.

**Continuous Learning Culture:** Annual learning is insufficient. SteelCore employees are expected to dedicate a minimum of **40 learning hours per financial year** to structured development activities.

**Digital-First Learning:** The primary learning delivery platform is SteelCore's Learning Management System (LMS). Blended learning (online + classroom) is the preferred approach for technical and leadership programs.

### 1.2 Annual Learning Targets by Grade

## SECTION 2: LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK

SteelCore's L&D framework is structured across four learning tiers:

| Tier | Description | Delivery Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 – Mandatory Compliance** | Safety, ethics, cybersecurity, regulatory | LMS (self-paced, annual) |
| **Tier 2 – Functional Technical** | Role-specific engineering, operations, quality | Blended: LMS + classroom + OJT |
| **Tier 3 – Digital & AI Upskilling** | Data analytics, AI/ML, automation, Industry 4.0 | LMS + workshops + projects |
| **Tier 4 – Leadership & Strategic** | People management, strategy, stakeholder management | Classroom + coaching + Action Learning |

All employees must complete Tier 1 programs annually. Tier 2–4 programs are role and grade specific.

Each department has a defined technical learning path organized across four competency levels. Employees are assessed against their current competency level and assigned development programs to advance to the next level.

## SECTION 12: INTERNAL WORKSHOPS AND KNOWLEDGE SESSIONS

### 12.1 Monthly Technical Knowledge Sessions (TKS)

Each department conducts a minimum of one 2-hour technical knowledge session per month covering:

- Lessons learned from recent incidents or process deviations.
- New technology updates and industry developments.
- Case studies from SteelCore projects.
- Guest presentations from equipment OEMs or industry experts.

TKS attendance is tracked in the LMS and counts toward annual learning hours.

### 12.2 SteelCore Innovation Talks

Quarterly company-wide sessions (in-person + live-streamed) showcasing:

- AI and digital transformation projects in progress.
- Best practices from other SteelCore plants.
- External speakers from technology partners (Google, Microsoft, SAP, ABB, Siemens).

### 12.3 Hackathons and Innovation Challenges

SteelCore hosts two internal hackathons per year:

**AI Hackathon:** Teams build AI prototypes to solve real plant problems. Winners receive ₹1,00,000 prize, project sponsorship, and fast-track visibility for High Potential program nomination.

**Safety Innovation Challenge:** Teams propose innovative safety solutions. Winners receive implementation sponsorship and recognition at the Annual Safety Day.

## SECTION 14: TRAINING APPROVAL PROCESS

### 14.1 Internal Training (LMS Programs)

- Employees enroll directly on the LMS.
- Mandatory programs are auto-assigned; optional programs require manager endorsement.
- Time-off for full-day classroom programs requires manager approval 10 days in advance.

### 14.2 External Training and Certifications

**Step 1:** Employee identifies the training and checks Approved Learning Catalog.
**Step 2:** Employee submits Training Approval Request Form on ESSP with:
- Course name, provider, dates, cost.
- Justification linked to IDP or skill gap.

**Step 3:** Reporting Manager approves or rejects within 5 working days.
**Step 4:** L&D Business Partner validates against budget availability and approves within 3 working days.
**Step 5:** Accounts processes payment or pre-payment within 10 working days.

### 14.3 Reimbursement Claim Process

1. Submit claim via ESSP Learning module within 30 days of completion.
2. Attach: original receipt/invoice, certificate of completion, pre-approval email.
3. L&D Business Partner validates within 5 working days.
4. Accounts processes reimbursement within 15 working days of L&D validation.

## SECTION 15: LEARNING COMPLETION AND ASSESSMENT RULES

### 15.1 LMS Completion Standards

- A program is marked complete when the employee achieves a minimum score of **70%** in the final assessment.
- Programs with practical or simulation components require a **pass score of 80%**.
- Employees who fail on the first attempt receive one free re-attempt within 7 days.
- A second failure triggers mandatory review with the L&D Business Partner and department manager.

### 15.2 Annual Learning Compliance

- Minimum learning hours must be logged by **31 March** of each financial year.
- Shortfall in mandatory programs blocks performance rating submission.
- Employees who exceed learning targets by 50%+ are recognized in the Annual L&D Excellence Awards.

### 15.3 Assessment Integrity

- Sharing assessment answers, using unauthorized aids, or having another person complete an assessment constitutes academic misconduct.
- Academic misconduct results in immediate disqualification, mandatory re-completion, and a conduct note in the employee file.

## SECTION 16: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q1: How do I access the LMS?**
> Log in to the Employee Self-Service Portal (ESSP) and navigate to "My Learning." The LMS is accessible on company laptops, tablets, and mobile devices.

**Q2: Can I complete learning programs outside working hours?**
> Yes. Self-paced LMS programs may be completed at any time. Hours completed outside of working hours still count toward the annual learning target and may be eligible for Learning Overtime allowance (applicable to Grades 1–4 with manager approval).

**Q3: What if a mandatory training program is not available in my preferred language?**
> All mandatory programs are available in English and Hindi. Regional language versions (Marathi, Odia, Bengali) are available for safety programs. Contact the L&D Business Partner for language accommodation requests.

**Q4: Can I request a training program that is not in the Approved Learning Catalog?**
> Yes. Submit a New Learning Request through the ESSP. The L&D team will evaluate it within 10 working days. If approved, it will be added to the catalog or a one-time approval will be issued.

**Q5: Will my certification renewal be funded if I obtained the original certification through company funding?**
> Yes. Renewal of company-sponsored certifications is automatically approved and funded, provided the certification remains relevant to your current role.
