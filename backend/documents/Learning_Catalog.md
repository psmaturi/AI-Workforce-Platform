## SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION

This Learning Catalog lists all approved learning programs available to SteelCore employees in the Financial Year 2025–2026. It is updated quarterly. Programs marked with (**M**) are **Mandatory**. Programs marked with (**R**) are **Recommended** for the target audience. Programs marked with (**E**) are **Elective**.

### Catalog Codes

- **INT** — Internal LMS program (self-paced, available anytime)
- **CLS** — Classroom / Instructor-led (scheduled sessions)
- **BLD** — Blended (online + classroom/practical)
- **EXT** — External program (vendor, institution, or platform)

### Learning Hours Counting Policy

All programs in this catalog count toward the annual learning target. Upon completion, hours are automatically logged in the LMS if enrollment is through the ESSP. External platform completions must be manually logged with a certificate upload.

## SECTION 13: HOW TO ENROLL

### Step 1: Access the Learning Catalog
Log in to the Employee Self-Service Portal (ESSP) → My Learning → Learning Catalog.

### Step 2: Select a Program
Browse by department, topic, grade, or delivery type. Use filters to find programs relevant to your IDP.

### Step 3: Check Eligibility
The system will verify your grade, prerequisites, and any mandatory completions required before enrollment.

### Step 4: Get Manager Approval (for CLS/BLD/EXT programs)
For programs requiring time off from work or financial investment:
- Click "Request Manager Approval."
- Manager receives an ESSP notification and approves within 5 working days.

### Step 5: Enroll and Complete
Once approved, click Enroll. The program appears in "My Learning Plan." Complete before the target date shown in your IDP.

### Step 6: Record Completion
- INT programs: automatically recorded upon assessment completion.
- EXT programs: upload certificate in "My Completions" within 30 days.
