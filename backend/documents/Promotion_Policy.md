## SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Promotion Policy Manual defines the criteria, process, timeline, and governance for career advancement at SteelCore Industries Limited. It applies to all permanent employees across all departments, grades, and plant locations.

The purpose of this policy is to:

- Ensure promotions are merit-based, transparent, and consistently applied.
- Provide employees with clear, unambiguous criteria for advancement planning.
- Recognize and retain high performers who demonstrate readiness for greater responsibility.
- Align talent advancement with organizational capability requirements and business strategy.

This policy applies to vertical promotions (movement to a higher grade). Lateral transfers, acting arrangements, and project-based role changes are governed by separate policies.

---

## SECTION 2: PROMOTION PHILOSOPHY

SteelCore's promotion philosophy is built on four pillars:

**1. Performance Over Seniority:** Time in service alone does not qualify an employee for promotion. Sustained high performance, demonstrated competency at the next level, and organizational need are the primary criteria.

**2. Readiness, Not Potential Alone:** Promotion rewards demonstrated readiness to perform at the next grade — not merely the potential to do so in the future.

**3. Transparency:** All promotion criteria, timelines, and decisions are communicated clearly. Every employee who is evaluated but not promoted will receive structured developmental feedback.

**4. Organizational Need:** Promotions are sanctioned based on business need and headcount approval from the Business Head and Finance. No promotion is guaranteed even if eligibility criteria are met, if an organizational vacancy does not exist.

## SECTION 3: GRADE STRUCTURE AND PROMOTION LEVELS

### 3.1 Grade Architecture

| Grade | Title | Track |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 1 | Graduate Trainee / Apprentice | Entry |
| Grade 2 | Junior Technician / Junior Engineer | Technical |
| Grade 3 | Technician / Engineer | Technical |
| Grade 4 | Senior Technician / Engineer | Technical |
| Grade 5 | Senior Engineer / Senior Analyst | Technical / Specialist |
| Grade 6 | Lead Engineer / Lead Analyst | Technical / Specialist |
| Grade 7 | Specialist / Principal Engineer | Technical Expert |
| Grade 8 | Manager / Team Manager | Leadership |
| Grade 9 | Senior Manager | Leadership |
| Grade 10 | Department Head / General Manager | Senior Leadership |
| Grade 11+ | VP / Chief Officer | Executive |

### 3.2 Technical Track vs. Leadership Track

Employees may choose to advance along either the **Technical Track** (becoming deep domain experts at Grade 7 — Specialist / Principal Engineer level, progressing to Fellow) or the **Leadership Track** (transitioning into people management at Grade 8). Both tracks are valued equally by SteelCore.

Employees choosing the Technical Track at Grade 7 may advance to:
- Grade 7A — Senior Specialist
- Grade 7B — Principal Specialist
- Grade 7C — Fellow / Distinguished Engineer

These are non-management roles with senior compensation bands equivalent to Grade 8–9 leadership roles.

## SECTION 4: ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

### 4.1 Core Eligibility Requirements

An employee is eligible for promotion nomination only when ALL of the following conditions are met:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum Time in Current Grade | As specified in Section 4.2 |
| Performance Rating | As specified in Section 5 |
| No Active PIP | Employee must not be on an active Performance Improvement Plan |
| No Active Disciplinary Action | No unresolved disciplinary proceedings |
| Mandatory Training Compliance | All mandatory learning programs must be complete and current |
| Competency Assessment | Must meet minimum competency scores as specified in Section 6 |
| Manager Nomination | Must be nominated by direct reporting manager |

### 4.2 Minimum Time in Grade

| Current Grade | Target Grade | Minimum Time in Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 1 | Grade 2 | 1 year |
| Grade 2 | Grade 3 | 2 years |
| Grade 3 | Grade 4 | 2 years |
| Grade 4 | Grade 5 | 2 years |
| Grade 5 | Grade 6 | 3 years |
| Grade 6 | Grade 7 | 3 years |
| Grade 7 | Grade 8 | 4 years |
| Grade 8 | Grade 9 | 4 years |
| Grade 9 | Grade 10 | 5 years |

> **Exception:** Exceptional performers who demonstrate outstanding achievement may be considered for accelerated promotion with a minimum of 50% of the stated time-in-grade requirement, subject to CHRO approval. This exception may not be applied more than once in an employee's career.

### 4.3 Additional Eligibility — Grade 8 and Above

For promotion to Grade 8 (Manager) and above, the following additional conditions apply:

- Completion of SteelCore's **First-Time Manager Readiness Assessment** (for Grade 8 only).
- Demonstration of team leadership experience (project lead, acting manager, or task force lead).
- 360-degree feedback rating of **3.5/5.0 or above** from peers and cross-functional stakeholders.
- No more than one instance of a performance rating below 4 in the last 5 years.

## SECTION 6: COMPETENCY AND SKILLS REQUIREMENTS

### 6.1 Competency Framework

SteelCore uses the Integrated Competency Framework (ICF) which evaluates employees across three competency clusters:

**Cluster A — Technical Competencies:** Role-specific domain knowledge and applied technical skills. Assessed via Technical Assessment Panel and demonstrated work output.

**Cluster B — Behavioral Competencies:** Communication, collaboration, initiative, analytical thinking, customer focus, safety leadership. Assessed via 360-degree feedback and interview.

**Cluster C — Digital Competencies:** Data literacy, AI literacy, digital tool proficiency, ERP/LMS system competency. Assessed via digital skills assessment.

### 6.2 Minimum Competency Scores Required for Promotion

| Target Grade | Technical (Cluster A) | Behavioral (Cluster B) | Digital (Cluster C) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grade 2 | 50/100 | 50/100 | 40/100 |
| Grade 3–4 | 60/100 | 60/100 | 50/100 |
| Grade 5–6 | 70/100 | 70/100 | 60/100 |
| Grade 7 | 80/100 | 75/100 | 70/100 |
| Grade 8 | 75/100 | 85/100 | 70/100 |
| Grade 9–10 | 75/100 | 90/100 | 75/100 |

### 6.3 Skill Certification Requirements

For promotion to Grade 5 and above, the employee must hold at least **one active, relevant professional certification** in their domain (e.g., CMRP for Mechanical, CAP for Automation, Azure AI for AI/Digital, Six Sigma Green Belt for Quality).

For promotion to Grade 7 and above, at least **two active certifications** are required, at least one of which must be an advanced or expert-level qualification.

## SECTION 7: LEADERSHIP COMPETENCY ASSESSMENT

For promotions to Grade 8 and above, a structured Leadership Competency Assessment is conducted:

### 7.1 SteelCore Leadership Competency Model

| Competency | Description | Assessment Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Results Orientation** | Delivers results consistently under ambiguity and pressure | Behavioral interview + PMS data |
| **People Development** | Actively grows and coaches team members | 360 feedback + interview |
| **Safety Leadership** | Embeds safety culture; leads by example | Safety audit record + interview |
| **Strategic Thinking** | Connects operational decisions to business outcomes | Case study + interview |
| **Digital Agility** | Embraces and enables technology adoption | Digital skills assessment + interview |
| **Stakeholder Influence** | Builds productive cross-functional relationships | 360 feedback + interview |
| **Change Leadership** | Guides teams through organizational change | Behavioral interview |

### 7.2 Assessment Process

Leadership competency is evaluated through:

1. **Structured Behavioral Interview (SBI):** 45-minute interview with a panel of two (HR Business Partner + Department Head). STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) format.
2. **360-Degree Feedback Summary:** Consolidated report from the most recent annual 360 cycle.
3. **Case Study Presentation:** Candidate presents a 15-minute solution to a realistic business / operational challenge and responds to 10 minutes of panel questions.

## SECTION 8: TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT PROCESS

### 8.1 Technical Assessment Panel

For Grade 5 and above, a Technical Assessment Panel (TAP) is convened to evaluate the candidate's domain expertise:

| Panel Member | Role |
|---|---|
| Technical Expert (internal or external) | Primary technical assessor |
| Department Head | Business context evaluator |
| HR Business Partner | Process facilitator and observer |

### 8.2 Technical Assessment Components

| Component | Duration | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Written Technical Paper | 90 minutes | 40% |
| Practical Demonstration / Case Study | 60 minutes | 35% |
| Technical Interview with Panel | 30 minutes | 25% |

### 8.3 Technical Assessment Pass Standard

A minimum combined score of **65/100** is required to pass the Technical Assessment. Candidates who score below 65 are not eligible for promotion in that cycle. They receive structured feedback and may re-attempt in the next promotion cycle.

## SECTION 9: PROMOTION INTERVIEW PROCESS

### 9.1 Standard Interview Process by Grade

| Target Grade | Interview Panels | Total Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 2–3 | Reporting Manager + HR | 30 minutes |
| Grade 4–5 | Department Head + HR | 45 minutes |
| Grade 6–7 | Department Head + Senior Technical Expert + HR | 60 minutes |
| Grade 8 | Business Head + HRBP + External Panel Member | 75 minutes |
| Grade 9+ | Business Head + CHRO | 90 minutes |

### 9.2 Interview Question Framework

Interviewers use the following structured framework:

- **Technical Questions (40%):** Domain knowledge, problem-solving, process expertise.
- **Behavioral Questions (40%):** Leadership situations, safety examples, collaboration scenarios, performance improvement examples.
- **Situational Questions (20%):** Hypothetical scenarios testing judgment, decision-making, and values alignment.

### 9.3 Interview Scoring

Each interviewer scores independently using the standardized Promotion Interview Scorecard (Form HR-PROM-SC-01). A minimum score of **70/100** across all panels is required to proceed to approval.

### 11.2 Off-Cycle Promotions

Off-cycle promotions are permitted in exceptional circumstances only (e.g., critical retention risk, exceptional achievement in a major project) and require approval from the CHRO and CFO. Off-cycle promotions are limited to a maximum of **5% of total promoted headcount per year**.

## SECTION 12: POST-PROMOTION OBLIGATIONS

Employees who are promoted are expected to:

- Attend a structured Promotion Onboarding program within 30 days of the effective date, covering new role expectations, revised KPIs, and enhanced responsibilities.
- For Grade 8 promotions: complete the First-Time Manager Program within 6 months.
- Maintain performance ratings at or above the threshold for the new grade in their first post-promotion appraisal cycle. A performance rating below threshold in the first year post-promotion may result in demotion review.
- Not apply for further promotion for the minimum time-in-grade period applicable to the new grade.

## SECTION 13: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q1: Can I self-nominate for promotion?**
> Self-nomination is not part of the standard promotion process. Promotions are initiated through manager nominations. However, you may discuss your promotion readiness and eligibility with your manager and HR Business Partner at any time. It is advisable to have this conversation at least 6 months before the nomination window.

**Q2: What happens if I am eligible but not promoted?**
> You will receive a structured developmental feedback session with your HR Business Partner and manager within 30 days of promotion decision communication. This feedback will outline specific gaps and a development plan to address them for the next cycle.

**Q3: Can I appeal a promotion decision?**
> Yes. You may submit a written promotion appeal to the HR Business Partner within 15 days of receiving the decision. Appeals are reviewed by the Promotion Appeals Committee (HR Head + Department Head's peer). The committee's decision is final.

**Q4: Does my promotion include a salary increase?**
> Yes. Promotions include a grade increment of a minimum of 15% of current CTC, subject to the compensation band of the target grade and Finance approval. Increment details are included in the promotion letter.

**Q5: What if my Technical Assessment score is below the pass standard?**
> You are ineligible for promotion in that cycle. You will receive detailed feedback on areas of weakness. You may re-attempt the Technical Assessment in the next promotion cycle. No minimum waiting period applies for re-attempt.

**Q6: Is there a promotion quota — a fixed number of promotions per year?**
> There is no fixed promotion quota. All eligible and assessed candidates who meet the criteria will be promoted, subject to headcount availability. However, Finance headcount approval means that the number of promotions in any given year reflects both merit and organizational structure requirements.
